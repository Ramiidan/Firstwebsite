How to make a pistachio milkshake

Two scoops, a splash of milk, and a sprinkle of pistachios. Watch how to make one glass.

Read the recipe
  1. Put 1 cup of pistachio gelato in a blender.
  2. Add ¼ cup of cold milk, cover, and blend until smooth.
  3. If the shake is too thick, blend in a little more milk.
  4. Pour into a glass, sprinkle crushed pistachios on top, and serve.
